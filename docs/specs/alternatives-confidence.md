# Spec: confidence and alternatives per recommendation

**Status:** not started · **Workstream:** Product & UI · **Stretch goal** (Reonic PDF p. 7)

## Problem

Planners fear a black box. For each recommended component they need to know how sure the system is, and what a realistic alternative would change.

## Behaviour

On the Design tab, next to each included component:
- **Confidence:** the model's probability of inclusion, shown in plain words (for example "very likely", "close call").
- **One alternative:** the most plausible other option, such as "without a battery" or "next battery size up".
- **Impact of the alternative:** the change in estimated yearly savings and payback, from the physics module.

Picking an alternative re-plans the rest of the design through the same engine (`Designer.design` with `locked={...}`).

## Data and code

- Confidence: `Design.probability`.
- Neighbour spread: `Neighbours.query` in `src/reonic/model.py`.
- Alternatives: generated by re-running the engine with one component locked to its alternative value.

## Done when

- Every included component shows a confidence and one alternative.
- The alternative's impact is shown, labelled "rough assumptions" until the constants are checked.
- An unknown input lowers the stated confidence rather than being hidden.
- Covered by a test that locks a component and checks the downstream change.

## Out of scope

Ranking many alternatives, and any probability that claims more precision than the data supports.
